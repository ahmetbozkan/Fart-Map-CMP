# Store listing / ASO

Inputs: `planning/product/Growth.md` (positioning, ASO themes, risks) and `planning/product/Product.md` (voice, pillars). For competitor or keyword research, delegate to the `growth-analyst` agent.

1. **Positioning:** "the world's funniest social map". Lead with the map, friends' reactions and leaving your mark. Sound effects are a feature, never the headline (App Store 4.3 risk).
2. **Keywords:**
   - core: fart map, fart, prank, funny map, social map;
   - long tail: funny sounds, joke app, map with friends.
   - Apple: title 30 chars, subtitle 30, keyword field 100 (comma-separated, no spaces, no repeats of title words).
   - Google: title 30, short description 80; keywords live in the full description (natural density, no stuffing).
3. **Screenshots (6–8),** each one message, big caption, real UI:
   1. living map with markers;
   2. logging in one tap;
   3. friends reacting;
   4. avatars and sounds (cosmetics);
   5. privacy ("your exact spot stays yours");
   6. rank tab teaser.
   - Sizes: iPhone 6.9" and 6.5", Android phone. Avoid misleading claims.
4. **Copy rules:**
   - on-voice humor, family-friendly crude (12+ / Teen);
   - no fake ratings, no "#1" claims;
   - mention free use and optional cosmetics.
5. **Localization order (proposal):** EN → ES → PT-BR → DE → FR → TR. Translate keywords natively; don't transliterate.
6. **Output:** write `planning/product/growth/listing-<locale>.md` with every field plus character counts. The owner pastes it into the consoles.
