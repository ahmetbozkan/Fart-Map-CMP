# ADR-0011: Territory Is Out of MVP Scope

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-06-20
- Source: user decision

## Context
Territory appeared across product vision and loop discussions but had unresolved mechanics and fairness risks.

## Decision
Do **not** include Territory in MVP scope.

## Rationale
- Mechanics are underdefined and high-risk for abuse/fairness.
- Territory is not required to validate Phase 1/2 core loops.
- Deferring improves focus and reduces roadmap risk.

## Consequences
- Territory features remain post-MVP and require a dedicated ADR before activation.
- Related references should label territory as deferred, not blocked for MVP delivery.
