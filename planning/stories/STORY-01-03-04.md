# STORY-01-03-04: Device location and "center on me"

Status: Blocked (location permission timing needs the owner's call; owner)   Epic: EPIC-01-03   Features: MAP-01, PRIV-01   ADRs: ADR-0023

## Goal
The user can center the map on where they are. Foreground location only, asked at a moment the owner chooses (see Decisions), with a friendly denied state that keeps the map fully usable.

## Scope
- **In (draft until the timing decision is answered):**
  - `LocationProvider` contract in `core/platform` (current foreground location + permission status/request), with Play Services Location (Android) and Core Location (iOS) implementations, plus a fake for tests.
  - A "center on me" control on the Map tab. It requests permission when needed, then animates the camera to the user's position.
  - Denied / permanently denied / location-off states, written in the product voice. "Open settings" appears where the platform allows it. The map stays usable at the default camera.
  - Purpose strings: Android rationale copy and iOS `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription`, explaining the map (Growth.md §2).
  - Analytics for the permission result, with no coordinates (Observability contract).
- **Out:**
  - Background location (never in Phase 1).
  - Using the device location to log a fart (EPIC-01-04).
  - Showing the user's own precise dot to others (never: precise coordinates are owner-only).

## Acceptance
Drafted after the timing decision. The timing changes when the prompt appears and what the first-launch camera is.
