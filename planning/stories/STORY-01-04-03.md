# STORY-01-04-03: Detailed log fields (date/time, avatar, flavor, intensity, description, sound, locked voice row)

Status: In progress   Epic: EPIC-01-04   Features: CREATE-03, CREATE-03.1 (steps 2–8), CREATE-03.2 (rule 2), CREATE-03.3, CREATE-04, CREATE-05, CREATE-06, CREATE-07 (free + owned)   ADRs: ADR-0016, ADR-0019, ADR-0020, ADR-0022, ADR-0024

## Goal
On the Log Fart screen a user can, optionally, set when it happened, pick an avatar and a sound they're allowed to use, choose a flavor and an intensity, and write a short caption. Every field has a default, so the quick log from STORY-01-04-02 still takes one tap. Each value lands in the fart exactly as chosen.

## Scope
- **In:**
  - **Date & time (step 2):** shows the screen-open time; "Change" opens the platform date picker then the time picker (Material 3 on both platforms); no max-date check.
  - **Avatar (step 3):** a horizontal row of the avatars the user can use: free and active catalog items, plus items in `user_entitlements/{uid}.items`. Preselects `avatar_free_bean`.
  - **Flavor (step 4):** a 5-step labelled stepper, Fresh → Hmm → Stinky → Toxic → Lethal, default 3 (Stinky).
  - **Intensity (step 5):** a continuous slider from Whisper (0.0) to Thunderclap (1.0), default 0.5.
  - **Description (step 6):** optional free text with emoji, one of the four humorous placeholders, a visible counter, stops at 280 characters.
  - **Sound (step 7):** "No sound" plus the sounds the user can use (same rule as avatars), default none.
  - **Voice row (step 8):** always shown locked: a waveform placeholder and "Premium — coming later". A tap opens an informational sheet (CREATE-03.2 rule 2); no ad option, no purchase.
  - **New `CosmeticRepository`** (`core/domain/cosmetics`) with a Firestore implementation reading `cosmetic_items` (whole collection, small) and the owner's `user_entitlements/{uid}`, plus bundled starter items as the fallback when the catalog is unreachable or empty.
  - **Submit:** the `NewFart` carries every chosen field; `log_submitted` gets the real `has_description`, `has_sound`, `avatar_is_free`.
- **Out:**
  - The unlock sheet, buying, rewarded ads and the launch (paid) catalog: EPIC-01-06. Locked items are modelled and drawn with a lock, but a tap selects nothing yet.
  - Sound preview playback, marker sound on tap and the "Play sounds on tap" toggle: they come with sound playback (EPIC-01-05, marker detail).
  - Avatar artwork: names only until the art exists (function first, STATE "Decided").
  - `log_draft`, keep-or-discard on back (-04). Voice recording itself (Phase 4).

## Acceptance
1. **Defaults.** Given a fresh screen, the state carries the screen-open time, avatar `avatar_free_bean`, flavor 3, intensity 0.5, empty description, no sound. "Let It Rip" without touching anything hands off exactly the quick-log `NewFart` of STORY-01-04-02.
2. **Options.** Given the catalog and the owned ids, the avatar and sound options are: active free items, plus owned items (active or not), usable; active non-free items not owned, shown locked; inactive non-owned items, hidden. Sorted by `sort_order` then id. When that leaves no usable item of a type (empty catalog offline, unreachable, only paid items active), the options are the bundled starter items of that type, all usable.
3. **Selection.** Selecting a usable avatar or sound sets it; selecting a locked or unknown one changes nothing. "No sound" clears the sound. When the options change and the selection is no longer usable, the avatar falls back to the default (or the first usable), the sound to none.
4. **Flavor and intensity.** A flavor in 1..5 is set, anything else is ignored. Intensity is clamped to 0.0..1.0; NaN is ignored.
5. **Description.** Text up to 280 UTF-16 units is kept as typed; longer input is cut at 280 without splitting an emoji (a trailing high surrogate is dropped). The counter shows the length out of 280. A blank or whitespace-only caption is sent as null; otherwise it is trimmed.
6. **Date & time.** The picked date (the picker's UTC-midnight millis) plus hour and minute become epoch ms in the device time zone; a future time is accepted. Pure helpers, tested across time zones.
7. **Round-trip.** Given every field changed (time, owned avatar, flavor 5, intensity 0.9, caption, sound, FOLLOWERS), `createFart` receives a `NewFart` with exactly those values. `log_submitted` reports `has_description`, `has_sound` and `avatar_is_free` from the selection (false for an owned paid avatar).
8. **Uploading freezes edits.** While `UPLOADING`, every field action is ignored.
9. **Voice row.** Tapping the locked voice row emits one `ShowVoiceInfo` event; the sheet shows informational copy only.
10. **Firestore mapping.** `cosmetic_items` docs map every domain field (id from the path, type, name, `is_free`, `is_active`, `sort_order`); an unknown `type` or a malformed doc is skipped. `user_entitlements` maps to the set of its `items` keys; a missing doc is the empty set. A read failure is logged; owned falls back to empty (fail closed: paid items show locked).
11. **Catalog parity.** The bundled starter items equal the free active items of `backend/catalog/starter-catalog.json` (ids, types, names, order).
12. `scripts/verify.sh --full` is green, and CI (Android, iOS, backend, secret scan) is green.

## Decisions
- **Locked items are modelled but inert until EPIC-01-06.** The options include active paid items as `isLocked`, drawn with a lock so they "stay visible" (compose-ui rules), and a tap selects nothing. The unlock sheet, `cosmetic_sheet_shown` and the paid catalog arrive together in EPIC-01-06 (ROADMAP: "locked items wait for EPIC-01-06's unlock sheet"). No catalog seeds a paid item before then (DataContracts §3.13: the starter seed accepts free items only), so no user sees an inert lock. Source: ROADMAP EPIC-01-04 line, CREATE-03.2 rule 1, tie-break "simpler, phase-appropriate".
- **Usable = the rules' `cosmeticAllowed`:** (free and active) or owned (`items` key present). Inactive non-owned items are hidden. Source: `backend/firestore.rules` `cosmeticAllowed`, DataContracts §3.13–3.14. `expires_at` is ignored like the rules do (subscriptions are Phase 4).
- **Bundled fallback.** If the catalog yields no *usable* item of a type (cache empty offline, read error, only paid items active, the free ones deactivated), the picker shows the bundled starter items of that type as usable, so the picker always has a valid choice and the first log never waits on a catalog read (Product.md L1 under 60 s, ADR-0022 offline). `StarterCosmetics` mirrors the free items of `starter-catalog.json`, checked by the parity test that already guards `StarterAvatars` (now derived from it). Source: DataContracts §3.13, STORY-01-02-02.
  - **What the fallback doesn't fix:** the rules still require the item doc to exist (`cosmeticAllowed`), so an environment without a seeded catalog rejects every fart after hand-off, exactly as it rejects profile creation today. Dev is seeded (`OwnerTodo.md` A, "Dev catalog seed"); prod is tracked as "Seed the prod catalog" (`OwnerTodo.md`, EPIC-01-06 code + owner run), which must happen before any prod release. No change here.
- **Owned-items read failure fails closed:** logged, the empty set is emitted (paid items show locked; the rules decide anyway), and the listener retries with backoff, so a transient error recovers within the session. Source: kotlin-shared rules ("fail closed on entitlement uncertainty"), plan review.
- **Locked-item tap deviates from create.md CREATE-03.2 rule 1 until EPIC-01-06** (flagged in the PR for the owner). The lock drawing is a few lines and lets EPIC-01-06 wire only the tap; no user can reach it before then.
- **Default avatar stays `avatar_free_bean`** (the quick-log default), not the profile avatar: no new dependency, and the user changes it in one tap. Source: STORY-01-04-02 Decisions, tie-break "simpler". No `last_avatar` preference: LocalData.md §5 defines none.
- **Default sound is none.** `sound_id` is optional (CREATE-03.3); a sound nobody chose would play on every marker tap later.
- **Description limit = 280 UTF-16 units, hard-stopped in the field.** "Soft limit 280 with a counter; backend rejects above 500" (create.md step 6). Counting UTF-16 units (as the rules do) keeps every caption under 500 even if it is all emoji; counting code points could reach 560 units and fail validation after the user typed it. The cut never splits a surrogate pair. Source: create.md step 6, NewFart.DESCRIPTION_MAX, tie-break "simpler".
- **Placeholder rotation:** one of the four prompts per screen open, picked from the open time (deterministic in tests). Source: create.md step 6 (the list); no timing is specified.
- **Date/time:** Material 3 `DatePicker` then `TimePicker` dialogs (Compose Multiplatform, both platforms). The picker returns the date as UTC midnight; a pure helper combines it with hour and minute in `TimeZone.currentSystemDefault()`. No max or min date (create.md step 2). Source: create.md step 2, compose-ui rules ("platform conventions may differ, behaviour may not").
- **Flavor stepper = a single-choice segmented row** with the five labels as text, so the level is never carried by colour alone; escalating colour is visual polish (deferred, STATE "Decided"). Source: create.md step 4, compose-ui accessibility rules.
- **Intensity is a Double 0.0–1.0** (the existing `NewFart` and rules type). Source: CREATE-03.3.
- **Sound preview and the "Play sounds on tap" toggle wait for sound playback** (marker tap, EPIC-01-05): there is no audio capability yet, and a toggle with nothing to toggle is dead UI. Source: create.md step 7, ROADMAP EPIC-01-05, kotlin-shared rules ("don't build what belongs later"). Flagged in the PR for the owner, and the EPIC-01-05 ROADMAP line now names marker sound playback, the preview and the toggle so they aren't lost.
- **Time picker 12/24 h** uses Compose Material 3's default for `rememberTimePickerState` (the platform's setting where the library reads it). DST: a picked local time in a spring-forward gap moves forward by the gap, an ambiguous one in the autumn overlap takes the earlier offset (kotlinx-datetime `toInstant(zone)`); both tested.
- **Voice row:** locked waveform placeholder + "Premium — coming later" opening an informational bottom sheet with one "Got it" button. No purchase or ad option (CREATE-03.2 rules 2–3). The sheet is opened through an Event (kotlin-shared rules).
- **Field order follows create.md CREATE-03.3:** where, when, avatar, flavor, intensity, description, sound, voice, visibility; "Let It Rip" stays pinned at the bottom, so the quick log is unchanged.
- **The catalog is read as a whole collection** (a handful of items), filtered and sorted on the client: no new index. `cosmetic_items` is readable by any signed-in user; `user_entitlements/{uid}` by its owner (rules unchanged).
- **Privacy and monetization implications.** No new data leaves the device beyond the existing fart fields; the caption is never logged (`NewFart.toString` already omits it) and analytics carry booleans only. Reads only: the catalog (public) and the owner's entitlements. No price, no purchase or ad surface is added; locked items are cosmetic only and every creation capability stays free (CREATE-03.3 checklist, Monetization hard rules).
